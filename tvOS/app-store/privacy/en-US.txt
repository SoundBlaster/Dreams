Dreams for Apple TV — Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 25, 2026

Dreams renders animated glyph rain and an optional clock locally on Apple TV.
No account is required. The app does not transmit your preferences or rendered
content to the developer and contains no advertising, tracking or third-party
analytics SDKs. Rain and clock preferences are saved locally and can be reset
in settings. Removing the app removes its local data.

Apple distributes the app and handles purchases according to its own privacy
policy. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Apple may process
store and diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and policies.

If you email support, your email address, message and attachments are processed
to answer your request. Do not send sensitive information. You may request
deletion of support correspondence held by the developer by contacting:
Egor Merkushev, egor.merkushev@yandex.ru.

This policy covers the tvOS app, not the separate macOS app or its agent
integrations. The public website policy also describes website hosting:
https://egormerkushev.ru/dreams/privacy/
